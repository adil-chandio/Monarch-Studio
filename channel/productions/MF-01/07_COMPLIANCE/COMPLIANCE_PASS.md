## Corrections made during this review (the WARDEN worked)

Two statements in the first draft were **not supportable** and were rewritten before render:

| Draft claim | Problem | Corrected to |
|---|---|---|
| "Firearms stopped a charging bear in about two thirds of cases." | The literature says **~76%** (Smith & Herrero, Alaska study). "Two thirds" understated and was inconsistent with the cited study. | "Firearms stopped a charging bear in about **three quarters** of cases." + stat card changed `2 in 3` → **`3 in 4`** |
| "A grizzly holds 35 mph across broken ground, uphill, for a mile." | 35 mph is a **sprint** figure. The sustained figure in park records is **25–28 mph for up to 2 miles**. The original conflated the two. | "A grizzly can sprint at 35. And park records show bears holding **25 to 28**, across broken ground, **for two miles**." Thumbnail C subline corrected to "27 MPH FOR SECONDS. 35 MPH SPRINT." |

**This is exactly why A07 has veto power.** Both errors would have been quotable, and both were the kind of thing a knowledgeable commenter would catch — costing credibility on the exact topic the channel is trying to own.

## ⚠️ Two conditions attached to this pass

### 1. Upload disclosure (mandatory)
The plates are synthetic. At upload, YouTube's **"Altered or synthetic content"** question must be answered **YES**.

This is not a penalty — the newest video on the live channel already carries a "Made with AI" label. The difference is that this episode carries four original layers and a sourced script, so the label sits on top of real work rather than replacing it.

### 2. Narration style disclosure (recommended, in-description)
The voice is synthetic. The description's "ABOUT THIS SERIES" block states that script, narration, illustrations and sound are original to the production. **"Original" and "human-recorded" are different claims** — if you want to be explicit, add one line:

> *Narration is produced with a synthetic voice, directed and edited by Monarch.*

Recommended, not required. No policy in force requires it at this time.
