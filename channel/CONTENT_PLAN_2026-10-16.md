Aaj channel ke paas **ek proven topic** hai (bears = 54% views), **ek proven format** hai (7–9 min counted long-form), aur **koi identity nahi** (About mein "KARMA CAM", titles mein AI-clickbait, Shorts mein 5 different channels ka mix).

**Nayi identity — ek line:**

> ### MONARCH CAM — *Caught on Camera. Explained.*
> Har video ka wada: **encounter dikhayenge, phir usay khol kar batayenge** — kya hua, kyun hua, aur agli baar kya karna hai.

Yeh wedge kyun? Kyunki:

**Series ka naam rakho: `CLOSE CALL FILES`.** Episode numbers. Fixed structure. Recognisable intro/sting card (3 second, koi lamba title sequence nahi).

**Language ka faisla:** Monarch Cam **English** hi rahe (existing library English hai, wildlife audience global hai, RPM bhi zyada). Urdunglish VO is channel par **mix na karo** — woh alag play hai (neeche §11 mein optional idea).

**P1 — CLOSE CALL FILES** (yeh channel ka backbone hai)
```

0:45–2:30  Encounter 1 → clip (30s tak) → freeze-frame → BREAKDOWN (20–30s: animal ka signal, human ki galti)
2:30–2:45  VERDICT CARD (on-screen graphics: distance / speed / time-to-impact — studio ka measured style)
            ... yahi loop 3–5 encounters ke liye ...
7:30–9:00  WO RULE: ek line jo viewer yaad rakhe ("Bhalu ke saamne bhagna = chase trigger")
9:00–9:30  Next episode ka tease (agla video screen par, begging CTA nahi)
```

**P2 — THE COUNTDOWN**
Wahi structure, lekin ranking ke through. Aakhir mein #1. **Argument ho, sirf list nahi** — "yeh #1 kyun hai, aur jo log X kehte hain woh kyun ghalat hain."

**P3 — ANIMAL vs MACHINE**
Comparison spine: animal vs train / elephant vs JCB / croc vs boat / shark vs cage. Har video mein physics + animal behaviour ka analysis.

**P4 — SHORTS rules**
- Ek Short = **ek moment**. 2 nahi, 5 nahi. (Naya "Bears… wait till the last one" Short mein 5 moments thay → 614 views.)
- Pehla frame = action ka peak. Koi logo intro nahi.
- On-screen text hook 0.5 second ke andar.
- 15–35 second. Sirf ek insight at the end.
- Description mein bolo: "Poora breakdown: [long-form title]" — Shorts ko long-form ka trailer banao.
- Pinned comment: wahi voting mechanic jo tumne use kiya (1–5 vote) — woh sahi tha, aage badhao.

## 6. Packaging rules (aaj se law)

### Titles — formula: `[Stakes ya number] + [Animal] + [Payoff]`
- ✅ `The Bear That Opened The Car Door — And What The Driver Did Wrong`
- ❌ `I Was WRONG About Bears — #1 Will TERRIFY You` (AI-slop pattern)
- ❌ `They Thought They Were Safe... Until The Bear Did This! 💀` (template pattern)
- **Rules:** max **1** emoji (ya zero) · koi `💀` nahi · koi "WAIT FOR IT" nahi · 70 characters se kam · pehle 40 characters mein asli hook.
- **Aaj ki galti:** ek Short ka title sirf emoji + hashtags hai — usay turant theek karo.

### Thumbnails — 3 elements, bas
1. **Asli animal ka photo** (AI-generated face **nahi** — woh slop signature hai), extreme close ya full-action.
2. **Ek 2–4 lafz ka text**, high contrast, same font hamesha.
3. **Brand corner mark** — hamesha same jagah (bottom-right), 40px.
- ❌ Koi arrow, circle, red box, shocked-face emoji nahi. Yeh 2019 ka style hai aur AI channels ka signature ban gaya hai.

### Descriptions — template khatam
- Purana boilerplate ("We do not fully own the material… section 107") **delete**.
- Uske jagah: asli **CREDITS + SOURCES** block (clip ka owner, link, licence/permission).
- Description ka pehla 2 line = asli hook + timestamps. Hashtags max 5, relevant.
- `"Daily Dose of Smiles"` wala leftover jahan bhi hai **hatta do**.

## 8. Production pipeline (studio ke saath)

Yeh kaam Monarch Studio ke tools se hoga — naya setup nahi banana:

1. **Topic + brief** → `brain/concept.py propose --topic … --format film` se 3 concept options. (WF-05 / MOTIONGO codex follow karo.)
2. **Script** → forensic structure (P1 ka template). VO pehle record, picture uske saath cut.
3. **Graphics layer** (yeh asli differentiator hai): distance/speed/timeline cards, map, freeze-frame arrows, verdict card. Yeh "original value" bhi hai aur brand bhi.
4. **Source register** → har clip entry (owner, licence/permission, date). Bina iske video publish nahi.
5. **QA (3-pass):** muted pass (kahani picture se samajh aati hai?) · audio-only pass (VO flow + koi hole nahi) · 2x pass (rhythm).
6. **Delivery receipt:** Drive id + md5 + source list video description mein.
