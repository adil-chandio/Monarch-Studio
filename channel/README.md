## The one-paragraph state of the channel (2026-10-16)

Monarch Cam has 3,830 subscribers, 2,147,271 lifetime views and 73 videos after 2 years and 8 months.
**84.3% of all views come from 5 videos**, **~94.7% come from long-form**, and bear content alone accounts for ~54% of the channel.
2026 has produced 15 Shorts and 1 long-form, August had zero uploads, and the newest long-form ("I Was WRONG About Bears", 27 Sep 2026) holds **16 views, 0 likes** with a YouTube *Made with AI* label.
Subscription conversion is **0.178% (1 sub per 561 views)** versus a 0.5–1.0% healthy range.

**Verdict: the channel is not dead — it is off-brand, off-format and off-policy.**
The plan fixes identity, returns to the proven long-form format, and moves the content into YouTube's
"compilation where the editing tells a story" allowance (permissioned clips + original narration + original graphics + real credits).

## Operating laws for this channel (quick reference)

1. **Long-form is the product. Shorts are the funnel.** (95% vs 5% of views.)
2. **Bears and apex-predator encounters are the proven topic.** New topic families are tests, not jumps.
3. **Every Short is a 20-second slice of a long-form** — never a random clip.
4. **Every clip needs a source row** in `CLIP_SOURCES.csv` before publish.
5. **≥ 3 original layers per video** (narration, graphics, analysis) — this is both the brand and the policy requirement.
6. **Never a zero-upload month.** One video a week beats a burst and a silence.
7. **No emoji-only titles, no typo titles, no hashtag-stuffed descriptions, no "we don't own this" disclaimers.**
