## 1. Pehle woh baat jo sabse zaroori hai

Voice note mein kaha gaya: *"viewers ke brain ko control karke, hypnotize karke, dopamine de kar rokna hai."*

Us energy ko hum **do tareeqon** mein se ek bana sakte hain:

| Jhoota hook (short-term trick) | Sach wala hook (compounding) |
|---|---|
| CTR aaj ubharta hai | CTR thoda kam, magar **asli** |
| AVD girta hai — log promise poora hone se pehle jate hain | AVD upar jata hai |
| YouTube 48 ghante baad distribution band kar deta hai | YouTube dobara push karta hai |
| Subs aate nahi (`0.178%` conversion) | Subs aate hain |
| Policy risk + demonetization | Library asset banta hai |

**Yeh morality ka lecture nahi — yeh mechanism hai.** Jo channel aap banana chahte ho (daily, tabahi, millions), woh sirf doosre column se banta hai. Pehla column aapko **wahin pahuncha dega jahan aap abhi khare ho.**

**Aur aapka apna proof:**

| Video | Hook type | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Heart-Stopping Bear Attacks Filmed Live! | Dramatic + **sach** | **955,681 views** |
| I Was WRONG About Bears — #1 Will TERRIFY You | AI-clickbait + AI narration | **16 views, 0 likes** |

Same channel. Same topic. Sirf honesty ka farq.

**Isliye crew ka law:** *Hook tension bechta hai — jhoot nahi.* Aur jo hook promise kare, video woh **poora** karti hai.

## 2. Hook ka rule (A03 aur A06 dono ke liye)

**Allowed (yahi karo):**
- Asli tension: "Yeh clip 4 second lambi hai. Usme woh galti hai jo zyadatar log karte hain."
- Specificity: "3 hikers, 1 grizzly, 40 second."
- Open loop jo video poora karti hai: "Aakhir mein pata chalega ke shisha kitna patla tha."
- Number jo sach hai.

**Banned (kabhi nahi):**
- ❌ Aisa dawa jo footage na dikhaye ("Yeh bacha **mar gaya**" — jab video mein kuch na ho).
- ❌ `You won't BELIEVE`, `WAIT FOR IT`, `#1 will TERRIFY you`, `SHOCKING` — yeh AI-slop ke signature hain.
- ❌ Emoji-only titles (`💀🤔 #shorts #bear`) — aapke channel par yeh mojood hai, yeh galti hai.
- ❌ Fake thumbnail (thumbnail ka frame video mein na ho).
- ❌ "5 minutes mein X seekh jao" jaisa jhoota value-claim.
- ❌ AI-generated footage ko real wildlife bata kar dikhana.

**Test (A01 aur A07 dono lagayenge):** *"Agar viewer poora video dekh le, to kya usay lagega ke usse dhoka mila?"* Haan = FAIL.

## 3. YouTube policy — jo aaj lagoo hai (July 2025 ke baad)

### 3.1 Inauthentic content (pehle "repetitious content" kehlati thi)
- **Allowed:** same intro/outro, lekin har video ka **apna** concept. Compilations **jahan editing kahani sunaye**.
- **Not allowed:** mass-produced / templated / "thodi si tabdeeli wali" videos; image slideshows; woh series jisme sirf naam badla jaye.
- **Channel par asar:** YouTube **poora channel** dekhta hai, ek video nahi.

### 3.2 Reused content
- Kisi aur ka material — **permission ke saath bhi** — agar apni original value add na ho to monetize nahi hota.
- ✅ Value add hoti hai: commentary, analysis, education, "editing that tells a story".
- ❌ Nahi: raw compilation, minimal edits.

### 3.3 AI disclosure
- Jab realistic content AI se banaya ya badla gaya ho to disclosure **zaroori** hai.
- Disclosure lagne se video marta nahi — **magar agar uske saath value na ho to marta hai.** Aapka 16-view video isi ka saboot hai.

### 3.4 Music/SFX
- Har track ka licence. "No copyright music" wala claim bhi verify karo — bahut se "no copyright" tracks actually claimed hote hain.

## 5. Clip sourcing — sirf 4 acceptable raste

| Rasta | Kab | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Permission** (creator ne di) | Trail-cam, hiker, wildlife channels | screenshot/DM/email `receipts/` mein |
| **Licence** (purchase/stock) | Pexels, Pixabay, Artgrid, Storyblocks | invoice ya licence file |
| **Creative Commons (CC-BY)** | jab aapko attribution dena ho | source page ka archive |
| **Public domain / government footage** | USFWS, NOAA, NPS type sources | source page |

**Aur mana hai:** "credit de dete hain to theek hai", "internet par mil gaya", "yeh viral hai to chalta hoga", "unknown source", "fair use, section 107".

**Sabse aasan aur behtareen raasta:** 20 creators ko message karo — *"aapka clip, hum forensically analyze karenge, credit + link aapko, aur video aapko bhejenge."*
Bahut log haan bolte hain. Isse aapko **woh footage milta hai jo kisi aur channel ke paas nahi** — yani uniqueness aur policy safety, dono.

## 6. Manipulation vs persuasion — line kahan hai

| Persuasion (allowed) | Manipulation (banned) |
|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | Jhooti dhamki |
| Tension aur release | Ghair-mojood khatre se darana |
| Pattern interrupt se attention | Aankhon ko dhoka dene wale flash/strobe |
| Emotional sach (yeh cheez asar karti hai) | Fake sympathy / fake crying / misleading AI |
| "Yeh jaanna zaroori hai kyunki X" | "Sirf 1% log yeh kar sakte hain" (jhoot) |
| Comment mein sawal (asli) | Engagement bait ("aao subscribe karo warna…") |

**Aasan test:** *"Kya main yeh cheez apne bhai ko aise bechunga?"* Nahi = mat karo.

## 8. Aur ek aakhri baat — "tabahi" ka asli matlab

Aapka goal sirf views nahi hai. Views woh cheez hai jo aapke paas **pehle bhi thi** (2.1 million) aur jisne channel ko 3,830 subscribers par chhoda.

Asli tabahi yeh hai:
- Ek video jo **library asset** ban jaye — 3 saal baad bhi views de.
- Ek viewer jo subscribe kare aur **wapas aaye**.
- Ek channel jise aap **10 saal** chala sakein, ban na ho.

Uss tabahi tak 4 raste hain, aur chaaron is file mein likhe hain:
1. **Sach bolne wala hook** (jo AVD aur subs deta hai)
2. **Apna original layer** har clip ke upar (jo policy safety deta hai)
3. **Rights clear** (jo channel zinda rakhta hai)
4. **Ek identity** — kaun hain aap, har video mein same (jo subscribers deta hai)
