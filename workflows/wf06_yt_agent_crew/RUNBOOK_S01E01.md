## Is pehli video ka real maqsad

Yeh video **100K laane ke liye nahi** ban rahi. Yeh **system ko calibrate** karne ke liye ban rahi hai.

Kamyabi ka definition is video par:
- ✅ Saare 8 stages gate par pass hue
- ✅ A07 ne pehli baar mein PASS diya (rights clear the)
- ✅ AVD ≥ 45%
- ✅ **Aur ek naya rule** jo `DECISIONS.md` mein gaya

Agar yeh chaar cheezein hui, to crew **10vi video par** tabahi layega — 1st par nahi.
Aur wahi asli plan hai.
