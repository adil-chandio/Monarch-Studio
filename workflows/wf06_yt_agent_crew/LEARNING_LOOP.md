# WF-06 · LEARNING LOOP (A08 ORACLE)

> **Yeh woh hissa hai jo aapke voice note mein missing tha — aur yahi sabse zyada asar wala hai.**
> Aapne kaha: manager sab dekhega, approve karega, improve karwayega. Lekin **asli feedback loop** tabhi band hota hai
> jab **publish ke baad ke numbers** wapas research mein jate hain. Iske bagair crew sirf mehnat karega — seekhega nahi.

## 2. Baseline (publish se pehle — A08 likhta hai)

```
VIDEO   : CLOSE CALL FILES #01
```

## 3. Teen snapshots

### 48h — "kya YouTube ne test liya?"
| Number | Kya batata hai |
|---|---|
| Impressions | YouTube ne kitna test kiya |
| **CTR** | thumbnail + title ka faisla. < 3% = packaging ka masla. > 5% = packaging chali |
| **AVD %** | content ka faisla. < 35% = hook/delivery ka masla |
| **First-30s retention** | hook ka faisla. < 65% = hook ne dhoka diya ya slow tha |
| Traffic sources | browse? suggested? shorts feed? search? |
| Top geography | kahan ke log dekh rahe hain (language/thumbnail ka faisla) |

### 7d — "kya yeh chali?"
- views/day curve (upar, flat, ya mar rahi hai)
- subs from this video
- comments ka mood (kya maang rahe hain — yeh agli video ka topic hai)
- Shorts → long-form click rate (agar < 1%, funnel toota hua hai)

### 28d — "kya yeh library asset hai?"
- lifetime views + kya ab bhi views aa rahe hain
- kya yeh **suggested** mein ja rahi hai (library asset ki nishani)
- next video ka suggestion

| Symptom | Diagnosis | Fix kahan |

## 5. Teen sawal (har 28-din note mein likhna zaroori)

1. **Kis second par log scroll karte hain, aur kyun?** (retention curve se — feel se nahi)
2. **Kaunsi assumption measurement ne ghalat saabit ki?**
3. **Kis cheez se naya rule mila?**

Har jawab → **ek naya rule** ya **ek naya library item**. Phir woh rule `DECISIONS.md` aur WF-06 doctrine mein add hota hai.
Isi tarah yeh crew 10 videos ke baad **aapke channel ke baare mein woh jaanta hai jo koi bahar wala course nahi sikha sakta.**

---

## 6. A08 ke liye zone ehtiyaat

| Situation | Kya karo |
|---|---|
| Studio analytics available nahi | HUMAN ko batao. **Bina numbers crew andha hai** — yeh blocker hai, chhoti baat nahi |
| Sirf ek video ka data | Poora rule mat banao. Pattern chahiye (kam az kam 3 videos) |
| Ek video zabardast chali | Copy mat karo — *kyun* chali yeh likho, phir usay dohrana |
| Ek video buri chali | Turant format mat badlo. 2 aur videos ka intezaar karo |

**Sabse bara khatra:** ek video ke numbers par poora strategy badal dena. Aapke channel ka data keh raha hai ke
5 videos ne 84% views diye — yani variation bara hai. Isliye **pattern > awaaz.**

---

## 7. Weekly crew review (A01 + A08, har Sunday, 30 minute)

```
1. Pichle hafte ki videos ke 48h/7d numbers (A08)
2. Kaunsa stage gate par first attempt mein pass hua? (target ≥70%)
3. Kitne revise rounds? (target ≤1.5 per stage)
4. A07 ne kya roka? (0 = A07 so raha hai)
5. Is hafte ka rule: ek naya rule likho
6. Agle hafte ka brief: woh rule usme lagao
```

Yeh 30 minute har hafte **is poore system ka sabse zyada value wala kaam** hai.
