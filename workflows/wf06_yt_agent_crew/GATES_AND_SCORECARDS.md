## 2. Manager ka 8-point rubric

Har stage ke artifact par (khaas taur par script, master aur package):

| # | Point | 5 ka matlab | 3 ka matlab | 1 ka matlab |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | 3s mein rok deta hai, asli lafz | kaam karta hai par lamba | "doston aaj…" |
| 2 | **Curiosity integrity** | curiosity sach par based | thoda over-promise | jhoota hook |
| 3 | **Retention design** | har 20–30s event, 3 re-hook | do jagah dheela | khaali seconds |
| 4 | **Payoff** | hook ka seedha jawab, quote-worthy | theek | payoff missing |
| 5 | **Brand fit** | Monarch ka tone, series ke andar | mix | kisi aur channel jaisa |
| 6 | **Policy safety** | 12/12 clear | ek shak | risk |
| 7 | **Execution polish** | measured gates pass | chhoti galtiyan | laperwahi |
| 8 | **Uniqueness (10x)** | naam-le-layak cheez maujood | thoda naya | generic — sab ne kiya hua |

### Faisla ke rules
- Koi bhi row **< 3** → **REVISE** (us row ka numbered fix).
- Average **< 4.0** → **REVISE**.
- **Uniqueness < 4** → **KILL** (generic content ka koi ilaaj nahi. Isi wajah se channel 561 views de kar 1 sub laata hai.)
- Average ≥ 4.0 **aur** sab rows ≥ 3 **aur** Uniqueness ≥ 4 → **APPROVED**.

### Score ka record
Har verdict `DECISIONS.md` mein. 28 din baad A08 dekhta hai: jo video high-score aayi, usne perform kiya? Isse rubric khud calibrate hota hai.
