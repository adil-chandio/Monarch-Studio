# 02 · Visual DNA and composition

## The observed world

The reference and retained V5/V6 direction use a luminous pale-lavender center, deeper periwinkle edges and subtle paper/linen texture. Subjects are grayscale or violet-tinted photographic cut-outs and impossible-but-readable object assemblies. Ground shadows, foreshortening, scale and orbital marks make the stage feel dimensional. Large condensed ink headlines contrast with small italic connectors.

The baseline ink is RGB **30, 22, 59**. V6's secondary violet is **76, 45, 113**; a depth-shadow purple is **112, 91, 173**. These are observed implementation values, not a mandate to tint every pixel identically. Preserve tonal hierarchy: the subject and the claim must win over grain, glows and decorative lines.

## Hierarchy before effects

1. Decide what the viewer must understand in this beat.
2. Give that beat one dominant subject or object relation.
3. Place the main word/claim where the subject leaves readable space.
4. Add connectors only when they improve meaning.
5. Use secondary props to clarify the metaphor, not to fill empty space.
6. Let movement hand attention from voice to subject to keyword.

Negative space is useful. Do not cover every quiet area with a label. Do not put a permanent UI band at the bottom simply because subtitles are available. A title can live above, below or beside the hero; its placement follows the composition, not a single fixed paragraph box.

## Subject integrity

Faces, hands, fingers, bow/violin strings, crutch tips, shoe silhouettes, hanging ropes and shadow contact points are protected details. Missing pale highlights are not acceptable “background cleanup.” Depth boundaries belong in empty background margins. A dark bag may share the exact purple of the font: color alone is not semantic segmentation.

Keep camera and subject perspective coherent. A ground shadow should stay attached to its subject; an orbit's rear segment should read behind the figure, not cut randomly through the face. A visual may be surreal while its local spatial logic stays clear.

## What is flexible and what is locked

Locked: actual temporal motion, clean editorial hierarchy, concept-led imagery, natural narration pace unless explicitly changed, no accidental social branding, current caption policy, truthful production claims.

Flexible with deliberate review: metaphor, subject, narrative setting, secondary objects, shot length, palette variants, camera direction, transition selection and emphasis words. The lavender family is the default reference, not an excuse to repeat the same props forever. A different format or writing system needs a tested adapter, not silent stretching or missing glyphs.

## Do not confuse three things

A still image with a whole-frame zoom is camera movement. Independently changing objects/layers or native moving footage provide subject motion. Newly modeled and animated geometry is actual 3D production. This case used supplied moving footage plus 2.5D compositing and 3D-style type; it did not reconstruct Einstein or the vehicles as new 3D models.
