# 05 · Motion, depth and transitions

## Preserve motion before adding motion

The repair retained changing reference frames in fourteen prepared clips. The clip is sampled across time and retimed to the spoken beat. New camera motion does not replace native changes of orientation, stride, prop position or gesture. The source-only motion diagnostic runs before the new captions; animated text cannot make a still-image source pass the creative review.

Frame blending provides a lightweight interpolation between adjacent native frames. It can create ghosting during fast action. Inspect those moments; consider safer source trims or a separately tested interpolation method rather than pretending every blended frame is perfect. The case retained some intentional source motion-blur/grid treatments.

## Near/far 2.5D maps

V6 introduced independent near/far inverse maps, a modest dolly/yaw/roll and gentle far-plane softening. Crucially, it did not key out pale foreground pixels. A constant-depth plateau covers the complete subject cluster, and feathering happens in empty paper margins. A boundary through a face, hand, hair or string can deform it.

Historical case plateau rectangles and per-shot zoom strengths remain in the unchanged V6 module. The reusable module accepts a normalized rectangle and camera parameters from each new shot. Do not carry the old brain/runner geometry into a different project by scene number.

Observed V6 strengths ranged roughly 0.025–0.09; its case roll amplitude was 0.65 degrees and far-focus sigma 0.8. These are restrained implementation values, not universal aesthetic commandments. Check frame edges and subject proportions at the strongest transform, not only at rest.

## Three short transition treatments

**Depth push, around 0.25s:** a conceptual escalation or move into a state. It briefly changes scale/roll and applies restrained radial blur.

**Diagonal whip, around 0.25s:** a fast causal or analogy hand-off. Translation and directional blur connect two moving scenes. Do not prolong it until both scenes become illegible.

**Feathered directional cut, around 0.22s:** a clean hand-off without a full white flash. It should feel like a designed change, not a long slideshow dissolve.

Select a transition for a reason. Blindly alternating every effect is not direction. The historical V6 renderer cycles treatments as an implementation detail; the reusable shot manifest lets the editor choose deliberately.

## Typography as a separate plane

Titles can enter with a brief perspective yaw and extrusion, then settle into a readable hold. The case used a 0.34s entry and stronger relief in V6. The main word must stay readable for most of its display time. Endless distortion, bouncing every syllable or effect-driven overlap with a face is not premium.

## Non-negotiable review

Review entry, settled pose, strongest transform and exit. Inspect foreground edges, shadows, strings, shoes, source-mask boundaries and type collisions. Use several closely spaced frames around known problems. A midpoint-only contact sheet missed defects in the original repair; dense inspection found them.
