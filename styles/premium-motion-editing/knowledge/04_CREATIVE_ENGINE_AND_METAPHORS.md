# 04 · Creative engine, concepts and metaphor grammar

## Convert an idea into a visual mechanism

Use this chain: **abstract claim → concrete subject/object → relationship → action/state change → consequence → attention hand-off**.

A good metaphor can be understood in one glance and becomes more meaningful through motion. “Efficiency rises while talent falls” is stronger as two opposing vertical systems than as two unrelated pictures. “Chosen resistance” is clearer when a carried weight visibly affects effort than when a bag sits on a static slide.

For every proposed shot, answer: What changes? Why does it change? What does the change mean? Which word should the viewer hear/see at that moment? What would make the metaphor misleading? If the only answer is “the camera zooms,” the scene needs actual subject/layer motion or a different concept.

## Internal concept selection

Develop at least three feasible concepts internally when the brief is open. Score argument fit, one-glance clarity, causal readability, independent-motion potential, novelty relative to the style's prior projects, and asset/rights feasibility. Select a concept because it serves the message, not because it adds the most effects.

Record rejected concepts and why. A new chat should not waste time rediscovering that an option is visually confusing, legally unavailable or impossible with the current toolchain. Use `brain/registry.json` to avoid repeating the same prop/world/device combination for every subject.

## Useful operations

- **Scale contrast:** make the operative object unusually large while preserving a readable relationship to the person.
- **Materialization:** give an abstract noun a tangible state: burning, deflating, being supported, becoming heavy, or rising.
- **Opposed vectors:** two systems move in different directions so the trade-off is visible.
- **Functional substitution:** place a machine where a human function would be, while keeping the critique about behavior rather than a person's worth.
- **Voluntary difficulty:** show a person choosing resistance despite access to convenience.
- **Compression:** combine time, practice and thought in one legible assembly rather than adding several slides.
- **Delayed consequence:** resolve the image but leave a meaningful question, not an unrelated logo card.

## Novelty without random surrealism

Surprise must still be interpretable. Limit the number of competing actions. Keep one foreground idea dominant. A viewer should not need to decode five unrelated symbols before hearing the next sentence. New idea seeds in `data/metaphor-bank.json` are explicitly labeled as suggestions, not previously rendered projects.

Novelty is not achieved by changing only a color or replacing Einstein's face. For a new subject, change the conceptual mechanism, setting or relationship while retaining the quality grammar. Reuse engineering components—camera maps, typography fitting, codecs, recovery—not somebody else's entire argument by accident.

## Motion planning worksheet

For each beat, specify: primary subject; secondary props; independent action; near/far depth plateau; protected details; start/hold/exit; emphasis word; transition reason; narration interval; sound event; and evidence of actual motion. These fields belong in `shot-plan.csv` before full export. A plan without assets remains a plan, not a delivered animation.
