## What this style is

A real 3-D animated short in 9:16 vertical. One cube per second builds a chrome and gold tower, and the 3-D text "30 HRS" reveals at the top. The metaphor is 20 minutes × 90 days = 30 hours. Hard cuts and whip-style angle changes. Narration: same script as the Tumhari Reserved Seat film, Roman Urdunglish, voice-02 (ur-PK).
