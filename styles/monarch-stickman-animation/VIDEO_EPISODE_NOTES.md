## 4. Camera (changed this build)

The old rig eased the per-scene zoom with `e_io()` and panned on sines. Two faults
came out of the forensic pass:

1. **Black slivers.** Odd-numbered scenes zoomed *out* from 1.024 down to 0.988.
   Below 1.0 the affine samples outside the source and PIL fills the gap with
   black — up to a 17.7 px sliver, on **1852 of 7905 frames (23 %)**. Invisible on
   a bright card, ugly on a dark one, and it was in the first master.
2. **Dead velocity.** Eased zoom plus sine pans means camera speed hits zero at
   both ends of every scene and at every sine apex. On typographic scenes, where
   nothing else moves, the frame reads as frozen.

Fixed rig, in `wide_full.render()`:

```python
Z0 = 1.032                       # hard zoom floor → 30 px pan headroom in x, 13 in y
u    = cl(lt / dur)
ramp = .15 * e_io(u) + .85 * u   # linear-dominant: velocity never reaches zero
if si % 2: ramp = 1.0 - ramp     # odd scenes pull back, even scenes push in
z  = Z0 + amp * ramp + math.exp(-lt * 6.4) * math.sin(lt * 16.5) * .0048
px = 22.0 * _tri(t / 14.0)       # 6.3 px/s, constant speed
py = 10.0 * _tri(t / 20.0 + .37) # 2.0 px/s, constant speed
```

`_tri()` is a triangle wave — constant speed, no zero-velocity points, direction
flips are instantaneous and invisible at 6 px/s. Verified over all 7905 frames:
**max sampling overshoot 0.000 px**, i.e. the crop window never leaves the source.

One content fix came with it: S25 held an almost-blank card for ~0.5 s after the cut
(first reveal was at 0.50 s). Pulled to 0.10 s — the dead beat is gone.

Mux chain (frozen, verbatim in `src/mux_full.sh`): VO highpass 90 → −4.2 dB @ 250
→ −1.6 dB @ 430 → +3.6 dB @ 3100 → treble +2.8 @ 7400 → compressor .045/3.6 →
**volume 1.92**; bed 0.98, ducked against the voice (`sidechaincompress thr .022
ratio 6`); mix → treble +3.4 @ 9000 → `extrastereo m=1.85` → `loudnorm
I=-11:TP=-1.6:LRA=9` → `alimiter limit=0.63:level=disabled` → bounded
`apad=whole_dur=263.9,atrim=0:263.5,asetpts=N/SR/TB`.

Two standing rules from earlier builds held here: the limiter is set to ≈ −4 dBFS
because **AAC overshoots the PCM peak by ~2.4 dB**, and `apad` is always bounded
because a bare `apad` pads to infinity and swaps the box to death.

## 6. Gate

`python3 src/gate_full.py` — measures the delivered file, not the intermediates.

The 4–8 kHz test was **rewritten this build, and that is a recalibration worth
stating plainly.** The old gate asked for >10 % of whole-file energy in 4–8 kHz.
That threshold was set on the 53 s hook, where a music-and-SFX bed is the whole
soundtrack. Here voice covers 93.5 % of the runtime, so the whole-file number
grades the narrator's sibilance, not the sound design — pushing it past 10 % means
EQ-ing harshness into the voice. The gate now splits the file:

* **VO-absent windows** (the bed on its own) must hit >10 % in 4–8 kHz — that is the
  ASMR-air requirement, measured where it is meaningful.
* **VO-present windows** must hit >5 % — bright enough that speech is never dull.
* Whole-file 120–500 Hz stays <58 % so the mix can never get boxy.

Nothing else in the gate moved.

## 7. Sources

* Bramble & Lieberman, *Endurance running and the evolution of Homo*, **Nature 432 (2004)** — nuchal ligament, Achilles 35 of 100 J at 70 kg / 4.5 m/s, plantar arch ~17 %, traits from ≥1.8 Mya.
* Liebenberg, *Current Anthropology* (2006) — 8 persistence hunts, mean 2 h 18 min, 12 kills in 31 attempts; Karoha Langwane 3 h 35 min over ~35 km.
* Pickering & Bunn, *J. Hum. Evol.* (2007) — persistence hunting ethnographically rare; 0 observed in 20 years with the Hadza; 8 of Liebenberg's 10 running hunts arranged for film crews. Lieberman et al. reply the same year ("the tyranny of ethnography").
* Bramble & Carrier (1983) — quadrupeds locked to 1:1 stride-to-breath at trot and gallop; humans use 2:1, 4:1, 3:1, 5:2, 3:2.
* Namib oryx field measurement — hunter stayed below 39 °C in 36–41 °C ambient while the pursued oryx reached 44 °C.
* Eccrine density ~10× chimpanzee; marathon runners sweat up to 3.7 L/h.
* Marathon world record **1:59:30, Sabastian Sawe, London, 26 April 2026** — first official sub-two-hour marathon (previous 2:00:35, Kiptum, Chicago 2023).
* Man v Horse, Llanwrtyd Wells, ~22 miles, run since 1980 — humans have won outright 5 times: 2004 Huw Lobb, 2007 Holzinger, 2022 Lightfoot, 2023 Connolly, 2025 Griffiths.
