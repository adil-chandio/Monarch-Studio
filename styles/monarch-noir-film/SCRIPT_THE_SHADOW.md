## Logline
A man and the shadow his own lantern throws on the wall. Every night it is bigger than him, so he hides in the corner — where it is biggest.
The night he walks toward it, it shrinks with every step, and when he touches the wall it is exactly his size.
The fear was never about him; it was about where he stood. So he puts the light where the fear was and sits beside it.

- When he **carries the light in front of him**, the wall is clean and the shadow lies on the floor behind him. When he sets the lamp at the wall's foot, there is no wall shadow at all.
