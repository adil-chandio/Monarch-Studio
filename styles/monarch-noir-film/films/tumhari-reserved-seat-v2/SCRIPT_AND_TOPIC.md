- Format: 1920×1080, 16:9 landscape
- Locked duration: **74.00 seconds**; V2 picture timing remains byte-locked to the accepted 34-beat voice timeline.
- Audience: Desi teens and young adults carrying family pressure, financial anxiety, phone distraction, delayed goals, and the fear of being left behind.

## Why this topic won

1. **Immediate FOMO:** “your dream-life seat will not stay empty.”
2. **Mass Desi relatability:** 11:47 PM, one more reel, ghar ka pressure, bills, confusion, postponed work.
3. **Specificity:** 20 minutes × 90 days = 1,800 minutes = 30 hours.
4. **Human scale:** it asks for 20 minutes, not an unrealistic overnight transformation.
5. **Two-style visual power:** WF-01 can turn time into kinetic diagrams and split paths; WF-03 can embody opportunity as a midnight railway ticket and departing reserved seat.
6. **Freshness:** does not repeat WF-01’s money/power/izzat topics or WF-03’s fear/comparison worlds.
