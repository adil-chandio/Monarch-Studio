## 0. What this format is — and is not

| is | is not |
|---|---|
| one everyday physical law, broken once, on camera, in one continuous shot | a montage of "AI effects" |
| the break *means* something (the metaphor names a human truth in ≤ 7 words) | a trick with a caption |
| physically consistent everywhere else (shadows, parallax, mass, contact) — that is why it reads as impossible instead of "AI-ish" | morph soup, floating objects without contact shadows, random camera |
| legible muted; sound is the reward for the unmuted | a video that needs the VO to make sense |
| loops: the last frame hands back to frame 1 | an ending card with a CTA inside the picture |
| 20.0–30.0 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, composed to survive a 4:5 centre crop | 60–90 s lessons (that is `short`), 2–4 min films (that is `film`) |

## 1. The three thumbs — the business logic of thirty seconds

Every WTF-30 is engineered for three decisions the viewer makes with the thumb, each at a known second, each with a gate:

| thumb | when | what must happen | why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stop-thumb** | 0.0–0.8 s | frame 1 already in motion; a visual question the eye cannot explain yet; no title, no logo, no "wait for it" | the viewer decides in ≈ 1.3 s whether to scroll |
| **Share-thumb** | onset 6.0–10.0 s (24–40 % of the duration), playing through ≈ 12 s | THE impossible moment lands, uncut, physically consistent, no text over it | "dekho yeh!" is a share impulse — it must have *started* before the scroll cliff at ≈ 12 s |
| **Save-thumb** | 70–90 % of the duration | the meaning snap: one line (≤ 7 words, one hot word) turns the impossible into "yeh toh mere baare mein hai" | comments and saves come from recognition, not from spectacle |

A fourth metric is free if the loop seam is built: **replays**. The ending rejoins frame 1, so the second viewing starts before the viewer decides to leave.

## 2. Identity constants (never vary inside the format)

- **Frame:** 1080×1920 @ 30 fps. The impossible moment and every word are composed inside the **4:5 safe centre (y 285–1635)**; hot words inside **y 300–1500** (platform UI at top/bottom).
- **Hero:** Monarch — SHADE rig (WF-01 paper/ink) or humanised Monarch (WF-03 noir). The hero is **always drawn by the rig**, never AI-generated per frame (identity lock, consistency, measurability).
- **World:** generated PNG plates split into ≥ 3 depth layers (foreground prop · hero plane · world), measured crop camera. One world per video; the world is burned after one use (registry rule).
- **Duration:** 20.0–30.0 s. Reference timing below is written for 25 s; scale the windows proportionally for 20/30.
- **Sound:** VO centred and de-essed if used (≤ 3 lines); bed = cues, no noise floor; **−14.0 ± 1.0 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.0 dBTP** (social platforms normalise to ≈ −14; louder masters only lose dynamic range — this is deliberately different from `short`'s −11.6).
- **Type:** Montserrat extruded, ≤ 7 on-screen words in the whole piece plus one meaning line (≤ 7 words, one hot word), every line ends with a full stop.
- **No CTA card in picture.** The CTA lives in the caption. The last frame is the loop seam.
- **The 19 engine gates, gate #20 freshness, and the 13 WTF gates (§11).**

## 3. The spine in seconds (25 s reference)

| window | beat | what the picture does | what the sound does | gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.8 | **Freeze-thumb** | already mid-motion (a fall, a turn, a light arriving); one detail the eye cannot explain | one onset cue within ± 1 frame of the motion; no music yet | G-W1, G-W2 |
| 0.8–3.0 | **The Normal** | establish the law everyone knows (gravity, a mirror reflects you, a clock runs forward, the floor is solid) with the hero doing an ordinary thing | room tone of the world; VO word one ≤ 0.6 s if VO is used | promise ≤ 3 s |
| 3.0–6.0 | **The Crack** | one small violation — the reflection blinks when he doesn't, the shadow is a frame late, the page edge lifts | a single wrong note / a sub tick; the bed thins | re-hook #1 (open loop) |
| 6.0–12.0 (onset 6.0–10.0) | **THE IMPOSSIBLE MOMENT** | the law breaks fully, physically, in **one uncut shot ≥ 3 s**; the camera is a witness (one motivated move), never a magician | **0.3–0.6 s of silence immediately before onset**, then one matched cue per physical event; ASMR precision, no stinger spam | G-W3, G-W4, G-W5, G-W6 |
| 12.0–20.0 | **Consequence** | the broken law has consequences inside the world; the hero reacts; one aftershock (a second, smaller impossible event or the first one evolving) | the motif enters once; cues follow objects | re-hook #2 at ≈ 50 % |
| 17.5–22.5 | **Meaning snap** | the line that names the metaphor (≤ 7 words, one hot word, full stop); the picture holds the proof, not a new trick | bed drops to one held note; the hot word gets the only "hit" of the piece | G-W7, G-W8 |
| 22.5–25.0 | **Loop seam** | the last shot resolves into frame 1's composition (match-cut or the same motion restarting); ≤ 1.0 s hold | end bell (the only recurring signature), then silence into the loop | G-W9 |

Density is measured in **events**, not cuts: something physical changes every ≤ 2.5 s (a light, a prop, a scale, a readout, a line) — but the WTF window itself is one shot. Cuts: ≈ 6 for 25 s; **never a cut inside the WTF window.**

## 4. The ten laws you may break (the taxonomy that makes "wtf" systematic)

Pick **exactly one**. Two broken laws is confusion, not wonder.

| id | law | the break (examples) | what it tends to mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gravity` | gravity lies | things fall up, sideways, or *toward* the thing that owns him | attachment, addiction, what pulls you |
| `scale` | scale lies | he shrinks per insult; the phone becomes a room; the problem is 10 m tall until he walks around it | self-worth, perspective |
| `time` | time lies | the clock runs backward only in his reflection; a year passes in his shadow and not on him | delay, ageing, routine |
| `light` | light lies | his shadow arrives early, refuses, or does the task; light casts darkness | the hidden self, fear |
| `mirror` | the reflection lies | the reflection finishes first, is older, walks out of the frame | potential, the better version |
| `material` | matter lies | paper becomes water; the floor is a page that turns; ink flows uphill | fragility, impermanence |
| `continuity` | space joins impossibly | one continuous camera move passes through an eye / keyhole / wall into another place | inner vs outer world |
| `count` | number lies | one hero becomes 365 heroes in one pan; a crowd folds into one | consistency, identity |
| `space` | geometry lies | the room grows with every "thoda aur"; the staircase leads back to its own bottom | loops, procrastination, traps |
| `causality` | effect before cause | the glass breaks, then falls; the stain appears, then the spill | consequence, regret |

**The consistency law.** The one broken law is the *only* lie. Everything else must be physically correct: light direction and shadow length agree, contact shadows exist under every foot and prop, parallax order is right, moving mass has anticipation and settle, cloth lags the body by 2–3 frames. The engine gates for jerk and luma steps apply inside the impossible moment exactly as elsewhere. Believability is what turns an effect into an impossibility.

**The no-cut law.** The impossible moment lives inside one continuous shot of ≥ 3.0 s. A cut lets the brain say "edit trick"; a continuous shot forces "how?!". The longest uncut shot in the piece must be the WTF shot.

**The never-explain law.** The trick is never shown twice, never slowed down to be understood, never captioned ("AI", "VFX", "wait for it"). The meaning line explains what it *means*, never how it was *done*.

## 5. Concept DNA for WTF-30

`python3 brain/concept.py propose --topic "<topic>" --dur 25 --format wtf30` → 3 briefs + 3 DNA JSONs, each already checked against the registry (blocked worlds, hooks, devices, signatures).

The eight slots and the signature move keep their meaning (see `MONARCH_EDITOR_BRAIN.md` §2). The format adds fields the build must fill before `gate`:

| field | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| `law_broken` | exactly one id from §4 | `"mirror"` |
| `impossible_moment` | one sentence: what is physically impossible and *when* it lands | "At 7.4 s the reflection puts on the jacket and walks out of the mirror while he is still scrolling." |
| `meaning_line` | ≤ 7 words, one hot word, full stop | "Tumhara best version intezaar nahi karta." |
| `wtf_onset_s`, `wtf_uncut_s` | measured onset of the moment and the uncut length of its shot | `7.4`, `4.6` |
| `beats`, `max_beat_gap_s` | physical events and the longest gap between them | `19`, `2.1` |
| `on_screen_words` | total words on screen excluding the meaning line | `5` |

## 6. How it is actually made here (the pipeline)

The honest engine: **AI stills + code physics + synthesized sound + generated voice.** This sandbox has no text-to-video model; it does not need one for this format. A video model cannot hold the Monarch identity, the exact physics and the gates for 25 s — code can, and code is repeatable and measurable. ## 7. Hook engineering for muted autoplay — the first 24 frames

- Frame 1 is a **motion already in progress** with a measured frame-to-frame difference (G-W1); never a logo, never a title, never a still.
- By 0.8 s one **visible wrongness**: a count that is off (two shadows), a direction that is off (steam falling), a timing that is off (the reflection is a frame late). The eye must register "wrong" before the brain registers "video".
- No words in the first 0.8 s unless the word *is* the wrongness.
- Visual-first hook archetypes (library, tagged `wtf`): `normal_then_wrong` · `impossible_in_progress` · `eye_cannot_explain` · `count_wrong`.
- The hook must contain the topic's own noun or object — a hook that could open a different topic is rejected.

## 8. The impossible moment — engine recipes (how each law is built, so it is exact and gate-measurable)

| law | recipe on this engine |
|---|---|
| gravity | a **radial gravity field** centred on the object that owns him (the phone): every prop gets a mass, a tether and a quintic-eased slide; cloth/hood strings lag 2–3 frames; contact shadows stretch toward the centre; release = all props drop with one thud |
| scale | hero scale keyed per trigger line with perspective-correct repositioning; **contact shadow and floor reflection re-sampled every frame**; the camera does not re-frame (the shrink must be felt against a fixed room) |
| time | a **time-offset buffer**: the reflection/shadow is the same rig rendered with t + Δ into a masked region; the Δ grows per beat; clock digits in the offset copy run from the same clock with the offset applied |
| light | light vector is a parameter; the shadow is rendered from the rig against the *same* vector for every prop except the hero's own; when the hero's shadow refuses, every other shadow still obeys |
| mirror | mirror region = masked re-render with reflected x and its own camera; divergence starts as a one-frame lag, becomes an action; the empty mirror plate is pre-rendered for the exit |
| material | floor/page = a **page-curl warp** over the plate (cylinder radius animated from the far edge); the rig rides the curl with a slide + grab pose; the page behind is a second plate (same room, one year later) |
| continuity | an **impossible oner**: camera path keyed through an aperture (pupil / keyhole / crack); the aperture plate carries an alpha tunnel; the second world is a depth-stacked plate set that is already parallaxing when the camera emerges |
| count | one rig, N instances with per-instance age/pose offsets, rendered along a measured pan; the pan speed obeys the jerk gate; the return to instance 0 is the payoff |
| space | **dolly-zoom made literal**: the far wall layer translates in z while the lens FOV compensates, so the room stretches and the door shrinks on screen; the Penrose loop is a door-wipe into the same staircase plate with a re-entry offset |
| causality | a forward particle/shatter sim rendered, then **played backward with a residue mask** (the stain stays, the ink returns); the sound is reversed with the picture, except the residue's cue |

Each recipe becomes a module and a registry signature; once used it is blocked forever (the signature rule), so the next video must invent again.

## 9. Sound in thirty seconds

- The sound tells the eye where the impossibility is: **one voice per physical event, within ± 1 frame.** No stinger spam, no riser bed, no noise floor.
- **A score is allowed (since #1 v2) only as a composed, event-locked bus**: its grid is anchored to the timeline (onset, hot word, moral fall on beats), it changes state with the picture (drop, breakdown, tape-stop into the silence hole, key change on the restore), it sits on its own bus under the sound design, and periodic SFX take its grid. The noise-floor gate is then measured on the sound-design stem; the silence holes stay digital silence.
- **The silence hole** (0.3–0.6 s) sits immediately before the WTF onset — the quiet is the drumroll.
- One motif only (the sonic identity slot), entering once in the consequence window and dropping to a held note under the meaning line.
- VO ≤ 3 lines, Urdunglish, human, measured first; or none (the muted-pass law makes VO optional).
- ≥ 50 % of SFX voices new to the catalogue; no cue repeated inside the piece; the end bell is the only recurring signature.
- Master: −14.0 ± 1.0 LUFS, TP ≤ −1.0 dBTP, AAC 48 kHz; resample explicitly after `loudnorm`.

## 10. Type and colour

- ≤ 7 on-screen words in the whole piece (excluding the meaning line); each hot word readable ≥ 0.9 s; never over the WTF window.
- The **meaning line**: ≤ 7 words, exactly one hot word, a full stop, inside the 4:5 safe centre, between 70 % and 90 % of the duration.
- One typographic twist per video (library slot) — the meaning line is usually the only place it is allowed to perform.
- Colour: identity palette of the chosen style; the accent arrives **with** the impossible moment and nowhere earlier (colour as an event).

## 11. Gates — the WTF-30 table (added to the 19 engine gates and gate #20)

| id | gate | measure | pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-W1 | frame 1 is in motion | mean abs luma difference frame 1 → frame 6 | ≥ 2.0 / 255 and `jerk_p95 < 2` |
| G-W2 | visual question by 0.8 s | event ledger has a `wrongness` event ≤ 0.8 s | present |
| G-W3 | WTF onset window | `wtf_onset_s` | 24–40 % of the duration (6.0–10.0 s at 25 s), never later than 12.0 s |
| G-W4 | WTF is uncut | `wtf_uncut_s`; no cut inside the window; longest shot = WTF shot | ≥ 3.0 s |
| G-W5 | physics consistency | checklist: light vector vs shadow angle, contact shadows under every grounded object, parallax order, cloth lag, anticipation/settle; luma steps inside the window | all rows ticked, `max_dluma < 8` |
| G-W6 | silence before the WTF | RMS of the bed over the 0.6 s before onset | a hole of 0.3–0.6 s ≤ −45 dBFS |
| G-W7 | muted-pass comprehension | `on_screen_words`; each hot word's on-screen time; no text in the WTF window | ≤ 7 words, ≥ 0.9 s each, none over the WTF |
| G-W8 | meaning snap | `meaning_snap_s / duration`; words; hot words; full stop | 0.70–0.90, ≤ 7 words, exactly one hot word, ends with "." |
| G-W9 | loop seam | perceptual difference last frame vs frame 1, or a logged match-cut | ≤ 6 / 255 mean abs difference, or a designed match |
| G-W10 | safe crop | the WTF object and every word inside y 285–1635; a rendered 4:5 proof | inside |
| G-W11 | loudness | integrated LUFS, true peak, noise floor in bed-only passages | −14 ± 1, ≤ −1 dBTP, floor below −60 dBFS |
| G-W12 | one law | `law_broken` | exactly one id from §4 |
| G-W13 | duration | master length | 20.0–30.0 s |

`concept.py gate` reads the DNA fields and reports G-W3, G-W4, G-W7, G-W8, G-W9, G-W11, G-W12, G-W13 together with the freshness rows; the remaining rows are measured by the per-video gate program (`gate_wtf30.py`) against the real frames and the mix. **Never weaken a gate to make a render pass.**

## 12. Series strategy and the mindset

- **A season of laws.** Ten videos, ten different broken laws, one hero, one grading family, one end bell. Anti-repetition is built into the format: the registry blocks the world and the signature forever and rests the other slots, so the series cannot repeat itself even if the editor is tired.
- **Same identity, never the same video.** The brand is the hero, the paper/ink or noir look, the type, the bell, the gates. The law, the world, the moment, the meaning — new every time, proven by the registry.
- **Wonder = familiar rule + one clean violation + honest consequences.** That is magic realism, and it is the only "AI creativity" people have not seen enough of; effects they have seen.
- **Cinema is not effects.** Cinema is a camera that behaves like a witness (one motivated move), light that behaves like a character (the accent arrives with the moment), sound that behaves like touch (one voice per event).
- **Never explain the trick; always explain the meaning.**
- **Measure, do not vibe.** After posting: 3-s hold %, average watch %, replays per view, shares and saves per 1 000. Three questions — (1) which second would a stranger scroll on, and why? (2) which rule did the numbers prove wrong? (3) what did we build that the library did not have? — each answer becomes one library item or one rule.
- **Cadence.** One WTF-30 per build cycle (≈ half a day each on this machine); batch look sheets for three at once so the user picks a week at a time.

## 14. Anti-patterns (what makes AI content look cheap — all banned)

- morph soup: more than one law broken, or a break that is not physically consistent;
- floating objects with no contact shadow; wrong parallax order; light that disagrees with its shadows;
- text or watermark over the impossible moment; "wait for it"; "AI" or "VFX" captions;
- the trick shown twice, or slowed down to be understood;
- a cut inside the WTF window;
- a riser + stinger instead of one cue per event; a music drop that matches nothing on screen;
- random camera moves; a camera that re-frames to hide the trick;
- an ending card, a CTA inside the picture, a logo bumper;
- a master louder than −13 LUFS; a noise floor; a silence longer than 1.2 s;
- a hook that could open any topic.

## 16. Post-mortem ledger (measured lessons from delivered WTF-30 films)

### WTF-30 #1 · THE GRAVITY OF THE PHONE · 2026-10-14

Full record: `styles/monarch-stickman-animation/films/gravity-of-the-phone/records/EXPERIMENTS_FAILURES_FIXES.md`. Rules promoted from it:

1. **Crest-factor rule (mastering).** If the mix's (true peak − integrated) exceeds ≈ 13 dB, ffmpeg `loudnorm linear=true` silently goes dynamic: LRA collapses and the target is missed. Fix the mix at source (slams ≤ 0.6, beds up), then master with linear gain + a look-ahead true-peak limiter (`build.master_linear()`); check the AAC overshoot. Dynamic loudnorm is banned for the format.
2. **Subject-anchor crop proof.** The 4:5 gate checks the actual subjects (phone, hero feet/head/pivot, text lines, from `render.camera()`), not an ink ratio — desk shots legitimately put legs and baseboard at the frame bottom.
3. **Contact sheet from the encoded master** (`select=not(mod(n,20))`, 6×5) is a mandatory review before gates. It caught an off-centre half-line that frame-by-beat checks missed.
4. **Pure-time scene solver, halves in parallel, half re-render.** 0.5–0.6 s/frame; a late fix costs one half (≈ 1.6 min), never the film.
5. **Two silence holes are measured, not assumed** — an inverted mute range is a silent no-op; the gate asserts both windows.
6. **Brain-gate schema:** `shots`, `pattern_interrupts`, `wtf_moments` are integers; `beats` must come from a real `beats_list` (here 38 beats, max gap 1.45 s).
7. **Rig conventions that cost time:** `shade.head_only(ang=)` is radians; toon `ar≈150` is arm-up, hand reach-down needs `ar≈0` + squat, hand floor ≈ 52 px above feet → anchor the feet, don't fake the reach.
8. **Hot word on the VO, not on the line.** Drop the whole line with the first VO word; let the hot word change colour/scale on its own syllable — the line stays centred and the slam lands on the voice.

Rules promoted from the **v2** upgrade (score · 96-voice sound design · moral · picture FX; same film, same day):

9. **Deterministic procedural randomness.** Python `hash()` is salted per process; parallel render halves disagreed at the seam. Use `zlib.crc32` (or any seeded, process-independent hash) for every speck, drop and jitter phase.
10. **Instant physics, eased picture.** A one-frame switch of a vignette or warp reads as a cut to the detector and to the eye (frame 369 was flagged inside the WTF). Release such looks over ≈ 0.2 s; a flash removal costs ≈ alpha/255 × (255 − mean) on the detector — keep impact tints ≤ 30 alpha.
11. **Score on its own bus, floor measured on the stem.** With a composed score the master never goes quiet; the "no synthetic noise floor" intent is measured on `mix_sfx.wav` (written before the music is summed). Holes are masked after the sum so they stay digital silence.
12. **Periodic SFX take the grid.** A free-running heartbeat against a 122.4 BPM score is mud; lock pulses to the half-time grid from the onset.
13. **Moral slot.** After the meaning line, ≤ 3 words on the VO inside the loop shot, snapped out by the returning hook sound; total on-screen words still ≤ 7. Never time-stretch VO to fit — move the cut (cut_s6 18.20 → 17.95).
14. **Desk-cam type layout.** The loop shot's wall has the lamp head at x ≈ 400–540 / y ≈ 750–880; type goes right-aligned (edge x 1012) and is checked by the subject-anchor gate on its own key frames.

Rules promoted from the **v3** upgrade (vocal-chop hook · kick-synced picture · 180° world roll):

15. **Whip-roll transition recipe.** Roll the finished frame with ≥ 12 sub-samples per frame (3 reads as a triple exposure), an exact corner-covering zoom `max((W|cos θ|+H|sin θ|)/W, (W|sin θ|+H|cos θ|)/H)` with a |sin θ| margin (peaks ≈ 2.0 near 60°, exactly 1.0 at 0°/180°), declare it as `ROLL_WINDOWS` for the cut detector (reported, never counted, never inside the WTF), end it with a planned snap-back cut, and time the loop hook after the hold.
16. **Visual side-chain.** The picture may pump (≤ 1.4 % zoom, ≤ 3 px chroma split) only on the score's own kick list; skip the kick on the loop seam so frame 0 and frame 599 share one zoom.
17. **Level a new instrument against the part it must beat** (the vocal chop needed 1.7 × to clear the arp), and measure an instrument alone before reading a mixed spectrum (the chop slot's 8.5 kHz centroid was the hats).
18. **Mirrored anchors on upside-down key frames:** map the 4:5 subjects through the roll; a key frame that lands inside a camera move aborts the proof instead of guessing.
19. **One grid for kinetic type and its ticks** (`LETTER_STAGGER`): letters and ink ticks land together; ticks are 12 ms band-passed clicks at ≈ 0.05 so VO stays clear.
